Faithwin Gbadamosi

My research uses GIS, remote sensing and spatial data science to understand how energy production shapes the environment and human health.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Geography at the University at Buffalo. My dissertation examines the nexus of energy, environment and health in the Niger Delta. I map oil spills and artisanal refining from satellite data, build contamination indices that rank places for remediation, and pair those maps with the lived experience of communities who live beside oil infrastructure. Before graduate school I worked in Nigeria's oil and gas sector.

I am also a Graduate Intern at the National Laboratory of the Rockies in Denver, where I support energy innovation programs focused on critical minerals, grid power and digital infrastructure.
