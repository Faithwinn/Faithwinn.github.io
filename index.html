Faithwin Gbadamosi

PhD Candidate in Geography, University at Buffalo (SUNY)
Graduate Intern, National Laboratory of the Rockies · Denver, CO

I use GIS, remote sensing and spatial data science to study how energy production shapes the environment and human health.

My dissertation looks at the nexus of energy, environment and health in the Niger Delta. I map oil spills and artisanal refining from satellite data, build contamination indices that rank places for remediation, and pair those maps with the lived experience of the communities who live beside the pipelines. Before graduate school I worked in Nigeria's oil and gas sector. Today I also support energy innovation programs at the National Laboratory of the Rockies, with a focus on critical minerals, grid power and digital infrastructure.
